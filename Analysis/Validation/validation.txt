Relatório de validações — Analysis ARVA

Base (DataAnalysisBaseRequest):
- batchId: NotNull (OnCreate); Positive (>0)
- notes: Size(min=3)
- startDate: NotNull (OnCreate); PastOrPresent; JsonFormat dd/MM/yyyy
- endDate: PastOrPresent; JsonFormat dd/MM/yyyy
- attachments: sem validação
- analysisType: NotNull (OnCreate)
- collectedBy: Size(min=2)

ConservationProgramRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- rccp, cscg, csp, practices, noDeforestation, carbonCredit, otherScope, biodiversity: opcionais (NotNull comentados)

CoverCroppingRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- cropType: NotNull (OnCreate)
- coverCroppingFamily: opcional
- isInterspersed: opcional

CropCorrectiveApplicationRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- correctiveType: opcional
- quantity: PositiveOrZero (>=0)

CropProtectionApplicationRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- unity: NotNull (OnCreate)
- productName: opcional
- rate: PositiveOrZero (>=0)
- cost: PositiveOrZero (>=0)
- pestControlType, applicationMethod, isForPestControl, isForDiseaseControl, isForWeedControl: opcionais

EnergyAndFuelRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- diesel, gasoline, naturalGas, lpg, electricityKw: Min(0) (>=0)

FertilizerApplicationRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- rate: PositiveOrZero (>=0)
- cost: sem validação (anotação comentada)
- unity, productName, applicationMethod, hasFertilityPlan, isPrecisionApplication, isVariableRate: opcionais
- n, p, k, s: PositiveOrZero (>=0)
- inhibitor, slowRelease: opcionais

HarvestRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- cropType, harvestType, unity: opcionais
- residueRemovalCount: PositiveOrZero (>=0) (int)
- residueBurning: boolean (sem validação)
- yield: Positive (>0) (float)

IrrigationAnalysisRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- method, unity, waterSourceType, irrigationCategory: opcionais
- quantity: PositiveOrZero (>=0) (float)
- drainage: PositiveOrZero (>=0) (float)

ManagementGrazingRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- stockingRate: Positive (>0) (float)
- animalType: opcional

PlantingOperationRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- seedsPerBeg: PositiveOrZero (>=0) (int)
- costPerBeg: sem validação
- unity: opcional
- brand: opcional
- plantingRate: sem validação
- cultivationType: opcional
- hybridVariation: opcional
- biologicals: opcional

PrecisionAgricultureRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- sectionControl, managementSoftware, opticalSensors, autotracAutosteer: opcionais

SoilPreparationRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- depth: Positive (>0) (Float)
- implementUsed, tillagePractice: opcionais

SoilSampleAnalysisRequest:
- baseRequest: @Valid
- soilSampleType: NotNull (OnCreate)

Notas:
- Em criação (OnCreate), sempre exigir baseRequest: batchId (>0), startDate (dd/MM/yyyy e passado/presente), analysisType.
- Tipos primitivos com @Positive (ex.: yield, stockingRate) não aceitam 0 — efetivamente obrigatórios com valor > 0.
- Muitos campos @NotNull específicos estão comentados; atualmente são opcionais no backend.
